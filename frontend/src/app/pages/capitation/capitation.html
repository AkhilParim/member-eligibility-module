Capitation

Provider capitation built from the MMR. For each provider contract, the members assigned to it are matched to their MMR rows for the payment month.

Flat PMPM: capitation = contract PMPM rate × member-months. A regular MMR row counts +1 member-month; a retroactive adjustment counts −1 (reversal, shown as a negative amount) or +1 (late add). MMR premium is shown for reference only.
